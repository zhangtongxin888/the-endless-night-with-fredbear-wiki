← Field manualFAQ / Tape 05

Short answers. Clear evidence limits.

Seven new-player questions answered only with facts published by the official game page or Roblox's official badge data.

01What is the main objective?

Explore the abandoned pizzeria and survive for as long as possible. The verified sources do not establish one fixed exit or universal completion condition.

02Can Fredbear run?

Yes. The official game description explicitly says that Fredbear will run.

03Which survival-time milestones are verified?

The official badge data confirms 1 minute (Survivor), 10 minutes (Nightmare Escaper), 30 minutes (Unbreakable Will), and 60 minutes (Living Legend).

04Can the power box be repaired, and what does it do?

The Power Restored badge confirms that a power box can be repaired. The verified sources used here do not establish its exact effect or repair steps.

05Can I become William Afton?

Yes. The game page and The Purple Legend badge confirm that becoming William Afton is possible. The verified sources do not publish how to trigger that state.

06What are FazCoins for?

The official description says FazCoins are used to buy gear. It does not provide a stable, complete gear catalog, so this guide does not invent one.

07Is the game still in Beta?

The official page labels the game as Beta and warns that bugs may occur and updates may be frequent. Recheck current in-game information after changes.
